Who we are

A garage, a workbench and more old cameras than we’d like to admit.

We’re a small family outfit working out of our garage in St. Louis, turning old cameras into lamps one at a time. No factory. Just us and a workbench.

From the garage

We didn’t set out to start a company. We set out to spend more time in the garage, and vintage cameras gave us a reason.

Every camera that comes through our door has already lived a full life. It took somebody’s birthday pictures, their road trips, their Sunday afternoons. That’s worth keeping around. So instead of leaving these cameras in a box, we give them a new job.

There’s no assembly line. Just one workbench, a jar of very small screws and a lot of patience. Every camera is numbered and stamped by us before it leaves.

When it does leave, it goes to a home where somebody will switch it on every night. That’s our favorite part.
