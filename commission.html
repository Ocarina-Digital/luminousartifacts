Commission Your Own Camera Lamp

Fill out the envelope just as you’d have left film at the corner drugstore. Tell us about a camera you already own, or the kind you’d like us to find. We take on only a few custom orders at a time, and every letter gets a personal reply. Which cameras work, and what we keep →

  1. Tell us the camera. Your own, a family heirloom, or one we find for you.
  2. Get a personal reply. A real person at the bench writes back with a few questions, a free quote and a date. Not a cent is due until you say yes.
  3. Get the camera to us. Mail it in (we send the address with your quote) or drop it off in St. Louis, by arrangement. If we’re finding one for you, skip this step.
  4. We build it by hand. Wired, tested, numbered and stamped in St. Louis, then delivered free around town or shipped to your door.
